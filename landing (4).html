What Makes PRAMAAN Unique?

Beyond Student Records.

Building a Connected Academic Identity.

PRAMAAN is more than a student data management platform. It connects students, institutions, State Directorates and AICTE through a unified APAAR-based ecosystem with transparent verification and academic mobility.

01

Explainable Student Verification

Identify duplicate records, missing information, identity mismatches and enrolment conflicts. Show the exact reasons behind every flagged record.

02

Cross-State Academic Mobility

Support smoother student transfers between institutions and states through structured academic record verification, with a planned integration pathway for Tamil Nadu UMS and EMIS.

03

Multi-Level Institutional Connectivity

Connect students, colleges, State Directorates and AICTE through separate role-based portals and dashboards.

04

Intelligent Compliance Monitoring

Validate approved intake, actual enrolment, supernumerary seats, NRI, OCI and foreign national categories against applicable rules.

05

Transparent Human Review

Provide officers with flagged records, review actions and audit trails to support accountable decision-making.

Built for the people who verify.

Three roles, each with access limited to what they need. The backend decides permissions, not the screen.

Institution admin

Keeps records clean at the source.

  • Uploads CSV or Excel records
  • Sees field-level errors with fixes
  • Tracks import history

Verifier

Checks identity and enrolment.

  • Reviews flagged and ambiguous cases
  • Sees evidence and the rule behind each flag
  • Confirms, dismisses or asks for more information

Regulatory authority

Monitors compliance across institutions.

  • Intake compliance by category
  • Anomaly and conflict dashboards
  • Reports and a full audit trail
